Learn Go by playing.

Called GOAUNTLET as a pun on go + gauntlet — the whole language, run back to back.

You're the gopher in the arena. Every round throws real Go at you — read it, run it, fix it, or lose a life. No setup, no accounts. A real compiler, lives on the line, and streaks that only count real work.

The game

  • learnGuided lessons with a real compiler. No lives lost.
  • quizFlashcards that cost lives and streaks when you miss.
  • speed60-second blitz. Answer fast, score big.
  • triviaCasual Go knowledge. Nothing at stake.
  • debugTap the buggy line. Two tries per case.
  • buildAssemble real programs step by step, decoys included.
  • orderPut shuffled programs into running order.
  • algoTen lessons, Big O to sliding window. Watch, then solve.

The rules

  • Wrong costs. Quiz misses take lives and reset streaks. Shields, hearts, and skips cost coins you earn by being right.
  • Practice is free. Lessons never take lives. Wrong picks there cost nothing and retries are unlimited.
  • Streaks mean days. Only real activity moves them. Opening the app does not count.
  • It really compiles. Run buttons use a real Go toolchain through a proxy, with the Playground as backup.
